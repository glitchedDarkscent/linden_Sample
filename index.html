    we open at seven with the oven already hot.

    Linden started as a tiny counter and a very good oven. The bakers arrive at five, the first loaves come out before the street wakes up, and the coffee comes from a small roaster we have known for years.

    We built the rest around how people actually use a cafe: somewhere to work in the morning, a long lunch with a friend, a glass of something cold when the day is over. Wi-Fi is free and so are the plug sockets by the window.
